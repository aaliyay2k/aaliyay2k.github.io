FinLens — financial statement analysis

From a messy annual report to a usable health view

Can a company’s filings be turned into a clear reading of liquidity, leverage, and year-on-year change — without making the reader do the extraction?

The questionAnalysts still spend long hours pulling tables out of multi-page or scanned annual reports before the actual analysis can start.
The workIsolate statement tables from unstructured filings, then evaluate liquidity, leverage, and YoY change into a plain-English health summary.
How to read itA first-pass view: what holds, what weakened, and what to watch — before a full model. Not a stock tip, and not a substitute for the filing itself.

Quiet tools: Python · Excel-style logic · PDF / OCR extraction
